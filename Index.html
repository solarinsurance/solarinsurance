Solar Insurance – Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive answers to every question regarding rooftop solar coverage, PM Surya Ghar integration, claims, documentation, and pricing.

1. What is Solar Insurance?

Solar Insurance helps protect your rooftop solar investment against financial loss arising from accidental and unforeseen events covered under a suitable insurance policy. Depending on the policy and coverage selected, protection may be available against risks such as fire, lightning, storm, cyclone, flood, earthquake and other covered perils.

2. Can Solar Panels Be Covered Under Home Insurance Under the Home Building or Home Contents Section?

Yes. Solar panels and rooftop solar systems may be covered under a home insurance policy under the Home Building or Home Contents section, depending on the nature, installation and structure of the asset. Since a rooftop solar system is generally fixed or permanently attached to the building structure, it may be considered for coverage under the Home Building section.

3. Who Can Buy Solar Insurance?

Homeowners with a rooftop solar system installed at their residential property may apply for Solar Insurance, subject to eligibility, underwriting requirements and applicable policy terms.

4. Can I Insure My PM Surya Ghar Solar System?

Yes. A rooftop solar system installed under the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana may be considered for insurance, subject to eligibility, underwriting requirements and applicable policy terms and conditions.

5. What Can Be Covered Under Solar Insurance?

Solar Panels, Solar Inverter, Mounting Structure, Cables and Electrical Equipment, and other declared solar system components or eligible fixed equipment at the insured home.

6. What Risks Can Be Covered Under Solar Insurance?

Fire, Explosion or Implosion, Lightning, Storm and Cyclone, Typhoon, Tempest, Hurricane and Tornado, Flood and Inundation, Earthquake, Riot and Malicious Damage.

7. Is Storm or Cyclone Damage to Solar Panels Covered?

Loss or damage caused by an insured event such as a storm or cyclone may be covered, subject to the policy terms, conditions, exclusions and coverage selected.

8. Is Flood Damage to My Solar System Covered?

Loss or damage caused due to flood or inundation may be covered depending on the selected insurance coverage and applicable policy terms.

9. Is Lightning Damage to Solar Panels Covered?

Damage caused due to lightning may be covered under the policy, subject to the selected coverage, policy terms and circumstances of the loss.

10. Is Fire Damage to My Solar System Covered?

Loss or damage caused due to fire may be covered under the applicable insurance policy, subject to the policy terms, conditions, exclusions and policy schedule.

11. Does Solar Insurance Cover the Solar Inverter?

The solar inverter and other solar equipment may be considered for coverage depending on the assets declared, their classification, the selected policy coverage and policy schedule.

12. Does Solar Insurance Cover the Complete Solar System?

Coverage of the complete solar system depends on the assets declared and insured under the policy.

13. What Documents Are Required to Apply for Solar Insurance?

Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, and either Solar System Invoice OR Exact Risk Location along with PIN Code.

14. Do I Need to Submit the Solar Installation Invoice?

If available, the solar installation invoice can be submitted to help establish value. Alternatively, you may provide the exact address and PIN Code where the system is installed.

15. What Is the Risk Location for Solar Insurance?

The risk location is the exact address (House/Flat, Street, City, State, PIN Code) where your rooftop solar system is physically installed.

16. Why Is the PIN Code Required?

The PIN Code helps identify the exact location where the solar system is installed and is required for risk assessment, policy processing and verification.

17. Can I Insure an Existing Rooftop Solar System?

Yes, existing rooftop solar systems may be considered for insurance, subject to eligibility criteria, underwriting requirements and acceptance.

18. How Is the Sum Insured for My Solar System Decided?

The sum insured should appropriately reflect the value of the solar system or assets being insured, determined via invoices and system valuations.

19. How Much Does Solar Insurance Cost?

Premium depends on system value, risk location, PIN code, coverage selected, policy period, and underwriting requirements (starting from ₹500 as per our matrix).

20. Is Solar Insurance Different from a Solar Panel Warranty?

Yes. Manufacturer warranty covers specified manufacturing or performance issues, while Solar Insurance protects against unforeseen physical loss or damage caused by covered risks.

21. Does Solar Insurance Cover Normal Wear and Tear?

Normal wear and tear, gradual deterioration and natural ageing are generally not considered sudden physical damage and are subject to policy exclusions.

22. Does Solar Insurance Cover Reduced Power Generation?

Solar Insurance is not a replacement for a performance warranty. Normal degradation or performance reduction does not constitute an insured loss.

23. Can I Buy Solar Insurance Online?

Yes. You can submit basic details and minimal required documents online for assistance with your insurance application and payment reconciliation.

24. How Long Does It Take to Get a Solar Insurance Policy?

Policy issuance depends on information completeness, document verification, underwriting requirements, and premium payment via UTR reconciliation.

25. Is a Site Inspection Required Before Buying Solar Insurance?

Inspection requirements depend on risk nature, property details, and underwriting guidelines. Not every application requires physical verification.

26. What Should I Do If My Solar System Gets Damaged?

Inform the insurer immediately, take safe photographs/videos, prevent further loss, do not dispose of damaged equipment prematurely, and submit required claim documents.

27. What Documents May Be Required During a Solar Insurance Claim?

Claim Form, Policy Details, Damage Photos/Videos, Repair Estimates, Invoices, Purchase/Installation Documents, and FIR/Police reports where applicable.

28. Can I Insure More Than One Solar System?

Yes, if systems are installed at multiple residential locations, each system and risk location must be separately declared with exact PIN codes.

29. Can I Renew My Solar Insurance?

Yes. Insurance protection may generally be continued through annual or long-term renewal, subject to underwriting terms.

30. Is Long-Term Solar Insurance Coverage Available?

Long-term policy options (up to 5 years) may be available depending on the selected insurance product and applicable terms.

31. Why Should I Choose Solar Insurance?

Rooftop solar is a long-term investment. Unforeseen perils like fire, lightning, and storms can cause severe financial damage. Solar Insurance provides an essential financial safety net.

32. Is Home Building & Contents Covered?

Yes, policies combine building structure, household contents, and permanent solar installations into one policy.
